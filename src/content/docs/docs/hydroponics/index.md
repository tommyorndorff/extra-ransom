Notes on growing food hydroponically through the winter in an unheated-ish garage — built around a 3D-printed [Vertical Garden: Large Hydroponic Vegetable Tower](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1343259-vertical-garden-large-hydroponic-vegetable-tower) ([archive](https://web.archive.org/web/2024/https://makerworld.com/en/models/1343259-vertical-garden-large-hydroponic-vegetable-tower)) from MakerWorld.

> The model page blocks automated fetching, so the tower details here come from a PDF printout of the page (captured 2026-11-01), summarized in [The Tower](/docs/hydroponics/the-tower/). The model's own page is the authority on parts and print settings.

## Pages

- [The Tower: Parts, Pot, and Pump](/docs/hydroponics/the-tower/) — what the MakerWorld design needs besides printed parts, pot and pump requirements, assembly order, print notes, and what its commenters changed
- [Tower Sourcing in the USA: Pot and Center Pipe](/docs/hydroponics/sourcing-in-the-usa/) — what US builders used for the 20 mm center pipe and the pot, with sizing checks, joining and epoxy notes, and print gotchas from the comments
- [Hydroponics 101](/docs/hydroponics/hydroponics-101/) — how a tower works, how often to run the pump and feed, pH/EC targets, weekly upkeep, and the best crops to start with
- [Seed Starting for Hydroponics](/docs/hydroponics/seed-starting/) — rockwool and plug starts, germination temperatures, lighting, and when to move seedlings into the tower
- [Garage Grow Setup](/docs/hydroponics/garage-grow-setup/) — lights, heat, timers, meters, and a shopping list for a winter garage
- [Continuous pH and EC Meter (ESP32)](/docs/hydroponics/ph-ec-meter/) — an ESP32 build that shows pH, EC and temperature on a small OLED; parts, wiring, firmware, calibration
