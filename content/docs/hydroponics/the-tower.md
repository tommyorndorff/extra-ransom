## What to buy besides the printed parts

From the designer's list:

- A large plant pot, **at least 40 L / 10 gal**, square, thick upper rim, **no drainage holes**, plastic (preferably polyethylene), with a drillable rim. The model is sized for a 38 × 38 × 40 cm pot; the page lists several European planters and notes the lid's STEP files can be adapted. A commenter points out you can screw the lid to a board and cut it to fit any pot, and another suggests an ordinary 5-gallon bucket works.
- A submersible pump. The designer uses a **Sicce Syncra Silent 2.0** (the 1.5 should also work). Another commenter designed a connector for a cheaper Amazon pump (MakerWorld model 1625658).
- **20 mm outer-diameter** sturdy pipe, 2 m, cut to length (designer used aluminum; one commenter used stainless).
- About **28 × M4 countersunk screws, 16 mm**, with nuts; 2 × shorter M4 countersunk, 8 mm, for the filter flap.
- **12 × M5 screws and nuts** for the base lid. Nuts go into the grow pots too: 4 per big pot and 2 per small pot.
- **3 cm aquarium foam**, cut into a filter pad for the lid.
- Net pots for the plants. One commenter notes that nuts can get stuck, and suggests embedded nuts instead.

The designer's growing setup: Terra Aquatica TriPart fertilizers, a pH meter and EC meter, filtered or rain water, rockwool cubes with vermiculite on top, then clay pebbles in the net pot after the roots develop.

## Assembly order, in short

The designer says **don't use an electric screwdriver or overtighten**: it cracks the printed and plastic parts.

1. **Base** — screw together the base lid parts (12 M5 screws and nuts), put the pump in the pot, and make sure the pump output sits **dead center** in the pot (measure it).
2. **Lid and filter** — route the cable through the square hole; drill two holes on each side of the pot to line up with the lid (they hold the rods); nuts in the small holes; foam filter with a center hole and a slit **facing away from the filter flap**.
3. **Structure** — the 2 m pipe goes in the center and must line up with the pump outlet; two rods go through pot and base; fill remaining lid holes with the cover parts; put the GrowBig4 on top.
4. **Final** — stack the pots, then the black sprinkler on top.
5. **Test** — fill the pot halfway. If no water comes out the top, the pump is too weak or the pump-to-pipe connection leaks (add a sealing ring). Screw the grow pots to each other, then fit the lids. If the water gets too warm, use the white cap on the top; **take it off on colder days to warm the water**.

To clean the pot, remove the top lids, pull the two base rods, and lift the whole tower aside so the pipe slides out; the plants stay in place.

## What commenters found

- **Algae at seams.** Each level snaps together, and one commenter got algae from water seeping at the seams, so they printed a cover that snaps inside to hide it. The designer says their own tower ran two months indoors on parquet without a spill.
- **Leak at the pump diaphragm.** One builder found the diaphragm isn't airtight and sealed it with gaffer tape.
- **Print problems.** A builder had trouble at the grow pot's connector; the designer says to use the **"BigTower with connector supports"** print profile.
- **Water temperature.** A commenter running two towers reports the high flow rate keeps the water temperature stable even in high ambient heat.
- **Add-ons.** A TPU cover cap for the plant holes (MakerWorld model 1526313) and a "Hydroponic tower light kit" from another designer.

## Implications for the garage

- **Pump:** the 180 cm tower needs a pump with enough head to push water 2 m up. Pick by **max lift**, and don't assume a cheap pump has it; see the shopping list.
- **Reservoir heat:** the sealed pot is a good place for a 50 W aquarium heater, and the white cap stays off in winter per the designer.
- **Freeze risk:** the pot's water is insulated by the lid, but the 20 mm pipe, pots, and net cups are full of water in cold air. Drain the pipe before a hard freeze.
- **Light:** 180 cm of tower needs lights along its whole height; see [Garage Grow Setup](../garage-grow-setup/).
