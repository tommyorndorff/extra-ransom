Notes on growing food hydroponically through the winter in an unheated-ish garage — built around a 3D-printed [Vertical Garden: Large Hydroponic Vegetable Tower](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1343259-vertical-garden-large-hydroponic-vegetable-tower) ([archive](https://web.archive.org/web/2024/https://makerworld.com/en/models/1343259-vertical-garden-large-hydroponic-vegetable-tower)) from MakerWorld.

> The model page blocks automated fetching, so the tower details here come from a PDF printout of the page (captured 2026-11-01), summarized in [The Tower](the-tower). The model's own page is the authority on parts and print settings.

## Pages

- [The Tower: Parts, Pot, and Pump](the-tower) — what the MakerWorld design needs besides printed parts, pot and pump requirements, assembly order, print notes, and what its commenters changed
- [Hydroponics 101](hydroponics-101) — how a tower works, how often to run the pump and feed, pH/EC targets, weekly upkeep, and the best crops to start with
- [Seed Starting for Hydroponics](seed-starting) — rockwool and plug starts, germination temperatures, lighting, and when to move seedlings into the tower
- [Garage Grow Setup](garage-grow-setup) — lights, heat, timers, meters, and a shopping list for a winter garage
