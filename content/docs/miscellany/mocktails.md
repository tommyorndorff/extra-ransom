Six no-alcohol drinks. The first three cover different ground — creamy/tropical, bright/citrus, ginger-forward. The second three (watermelon mojito, virgin paloma, mango chili-lime) were picked to travel: each adds just one new bottle to the cooler and reuses lime, mint, agave, and soda already on the list below, rather than needing its own dedicated shopping trip. No expertise assumed on my end either — these are straightforward builds, not bartending theory.

## Tools and stock worth having on hand

- Cocktail shaker (or any lidded jar/container that seals — a mason jar works)
- Jigger or a set of measuring shot glasses (1 oz / 2 oz)
- Citrus juicer or reamer — fresh juice matters a lot more than bottled here, especially for lime
- Muddler (the back of a wooden spoon works in a pinch) — for mint
- Fine strainer, if shaking anything with pulp or muddled mint
- Copper mugs, if leaning into the mule presentation — not required, just traditional
- Full-fat coconut milk (canned, not the carton beverage) and coconut water are different ingredients below — don't swap one for the other
- A bottle of good non-alcoholic ginger beer (strong ginger bite, well-carbonated) and a bottle of club soda or seltzer
- Agave syrup (or 1:1 agave-and-water simple syrup) — shared by the citrus spritz, the paloma, and the mango chili-lime below
- Coarse salt or Tajín, for rimming glasses — one small shaker covers the paloma and the mango chili-lime

## Packing these for the 5-hour drive down

This is a drive to a beach house with its own kitchen and freezer, not a cooler-only day trip — so the plan is simpler than packing for a walk to the sand:

- **Ice and blending both happen on arrival**, not in the car. No need to haul ice down — the house has a freezer, and the blender/muddler work is easiest done in an actual kitchen anyway.
- **Bring whole watermelons and mangoes**, not pre-juiced bottles. Whole fruit travels fine at room temperature for 5 hours and blends better fresh at the house than juice that's been sitting since before you left — this also skips worrying about fresh watermelon/mango juice oxidizing or losing flavor over the drive, which was a real concern for pre-blending, and isn't one for whole fruit. Bottled watermelon juice or mango nectar are still fine fallbacks if you'd rather not deal with a whole watermelon at all.
- **Pre-mix the chili-lime salt** in a small shaker or travel container (or just bring a bottle of Tajín) — no different whether you're rimming a glass at home or at the beach house.
- Cans of soda/ginger beer over open bottles — they hold carbonation better over a long drive and don't go flat if the trip is bumpy.
- Fresh lime, grapefruit, and blood orange juice travel fine for 5 hours if juiced ahead and kept in the car's AC rather than a hot trunk; agave, salt, and canned coconut milk don't need any special handling at all.

## Coconut Colada

Creamy and tropical — the coconut mocktail to reach for over a piña colada. Full-fat canned coconut milk gives real body; coconut water alone comes out thin.

**Ingredients (1 drink):**
- 2 oz full-fat coconut milk (canned, shaken well first)
- 3 oz pineapple juice
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- Splash of club soda or sparkling water, to top
- Ice
- Garnish: pineapple wedge or wheel, mint sprig

**Method:** Shake the coconut milk, pineapple juice, and lime juice hard with ice — the coconut milk wants the agitation to emulsify and get that piña-colada texture. Strain into a glass over fresh ice, top with a splash of soda, garnish.

*Variation:* swap the club soda for a splash of ginger beer if you want more spice against the coconut.

Source: [Be Well Table — Pineapple Coconut Mocktail](https://bewelltable.com/easy-pineapple-coconut-mocktail-recipe-non-alcoholic/).

## Blood Orange Citrus Spritz

Bright and a little tart — blood orange juice has more depth than regular orange juice, and the color is the best-looking drink of the three if blood oranges are in season (roughly winter into early spring in most US markets; regular orange juice works fine off-season, just less dramatic).

**Ingredients (1 drink):**
- 3 oz blood orange juice (fresh-squeezed if you can get blood oranges; bottled is fine otherwise)
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.5 oz agave syrup or simple syrup, to taste
- Sparkling water, to top
- Ice
- Garnish: orange wheel or twist

**Method:** Combine the juice, lime, and agave in a glass over ice, stir, then top with sparkling water. No shaking needed — this one's built, not mixed.

Source: [One Lovely Life — Blood Orange Mocktail](https://www.onelovelylife.com/blood-orange-mocktail/).

## The Not-a-Mule

A Moscow mule's whole appeal is ginger heat against lime and fizz — none of that depends on the vodka, so dropping it loses less than you'd think. This is the one worth serving in a copper mug if you own them; the cold-metal thing is real, not just presentation.

**Ingredients (1 drink):**
- 4 oz good non-alcoholic ginger beer (strong ginger, well-carbonated — the quality of the ginger beer is the whole drink here)
- 0.75–1 oz fresh lime juice
- Splash of club soda, optional, for extra fizz if the ginger beer alone isn't lively enough
- Ice
- Garnish: lime wheel, mint sprig

**Method:** Fill a copper mug (or any glass) most of the way with ice. Add lime juice, top with ginger beer, stir gently — stir, don't shake, or the ginger beer goes flat. Top with a splash of club soda if you want more sparkle. Garnish and serve immediately.

Source: [Yummy Mummy Kitchen — Virgin Moscow Mule](https://www.yummymummykitchen.com/2019/12/moscow-mule-mocktail-recipe.html), [Whisk Affair — Virgin Moscow Mule](https://www.whiskaffair.com/virgin-moscow-mule-recipe/).

## Watermelon Mojito (beach house-ready)

Reuses the lime and mint already on the list, plus club soda you're already packing for the Not-a-Mule. The only new item is a whole watermelon — bring the fruit itself and blend at the house, rather than pre-juicing for the drive.

**Ingredients (1 drink):**
- 3 oz watermelon juice (blended fresh from the whole fruit and strained, or bottled if you'd rather skip the blender entirely)
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- 4–5 mint leaves
- Club soda or sparkling water, to top
- Ice
- Garnish: mint sprig, watermelon wedge

**Method:** At the house — cube the watermelon, blend, strain out the pulp if you want a cleaner drink. Muddle the mint gently in the bottom of a glass, add lime juice and watermelon juice, fill with ice, top with soda, stir once.

Source: [The Merry Thought — Watermelon Mojito Mocktail](https://themerrythought.com/recipes/watermelon-mojito-mocktail/), [The Real Food Dietitians — Watermelon Mojito](https://therealfooddietitians.com/watermelon-mojito/).

## Virgin Paloma (beach house-ready)

Same lime juice and agave as the Blood Orange Citrus Spritz, same sparkling water as everything else on this page — the only new bottle is grapefruit juice.

**Ingredients (1 drink):**
- 3 oz grapefruit juice (fresh-squeezed if you're juicing at home the night before; bottled is fine)
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.5 oz agave syrup, to taste
- Sparkling water, to top
- Ice
- Salt (or Tajín) for the rim
- Garnish: lime or grapefruit wedge

**Method:** Rim a glass with lime juice and dip in salt or Tajín, combine grapefruit juice, lime juice, and agave over ice, top with sparkling water, stir.

Source: [Sweets & Thank You — Virgin Paloma](https://sweetsandthankyou.com/grapefruit-mocktail/), [Mingle Mocktails — Paloma Mocktail](https://www.minglemocktails.com/blogs/mocktail-recipes/paloma-mocktail-recipe).

## Mango Chili-Lime (beach house-ready)

Reuses the lime, mint, and salt/Tajín already on the list — a whole mango (or bottled mango nectar, if you'd rather skip peeling and pureeing one) is the one new thing to bring.

**Ingredients (1 drink):**
- 3 oz mango puree (blended fresh from a whole mango) or mango nectar
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- 3–4 mint leaves
- Sparkling water or club soda, to top
- Ice
- Chili-lime salt (Tajín, or your own chili/salt/sugar mix) for the rim
- Garnish: lime wedge, mint sprig

**Method:** Rim the glass with lime juice and chili-lime salt, same as the paloma. Muddle or tear the mint into the glass, add lime juice and mango puree, fill with ice, top with soda, stir.

Source: [Aura Bora — Mango Chili Lime Mocktail](https://aurabora.com/blogs/recipes/mango-chili-lime-mocktail), [Whole30 — Mango-Chili-Lime Mocktail](https://whole30.com/recipes/mango-chili-lime-mocktail/).
