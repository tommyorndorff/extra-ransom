Soft, chewy, four-ingredient dog treats made from ripe banana, oats, and an egg. They are a good counterpart to the [crunchy pumpkin biscuits](dog-biscuits-pumpkin-peanut-butter): no nut butter, no rolling out, and they mix in a single bowl. Makes about **2 dozen** treats.

> **Check before you start.** Use plain rolled oats (not instant packets with sugar or flavoring) and nothing else added. If you add a spoonful of peanut butter, make sure it is **xylitol-free**.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 2 | very ripe bananas (about 1 cup mashed) |
| 1 | large egg |
| 2 cups | old-fashioned rolled oats |
| ½ cup | oat flour (or whole wheat flour), plus a little extra if needed |
| 1 tsp | ground cinnamon (optional) |

## Equipment

- Large bowl and a fork or potato masher
- Spoon or cookie scoop
- Parchment-lined baking sheet

## Method

1. **Heat the oven to 350°F** and line a baking sheet with parchment.
2. **Mash the bananas** in the bowl until smooth. Stir in the egg and cinnamon.
3. **Stir in the oats and oat flour** until the mixture is thick and sticky and holds together. If it is too wet to scoop, add oat flour a tablespoon at a time.
4. **Scoop tablespoon-size mounds** onto the sheet and press each flat to about ½ inch thick. They won't spread much.
5. **Bake 15–18 minutes**, until firm and lightly golden on the edges.
6. **Cool on the sheet** for a few minutes, then move to a rack. They stay soft in the middle; for a firmer chew, leave them in the turned-off oven for 20 minutes.

## Storage

These are moist, so they keep less well than dry biscuits:

- Refrigerator, airtight, up to **1 week**.
- Freezer, up to **3 months**. Freeze them flat on a tray, then bag them; they thaw in a few minutes.

## Notes

- Bananas are high in natural sugar, so keep these to a treat or two a day, and fewer for small dogs.
- Treats should stay under roughly 10% of a dog's daily calories.
- If your dog has a grain or egg sensitivity, or you aren't sure these ingredients are right for them, check with your vet.
- Skip raisins, chocolate, onion, garlic, and xylitol in any variation of this recipe; all are harmful to dogs.
