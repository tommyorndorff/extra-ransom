A cozy vegetarian soup built on a celery-onion-carrot base, with canned tomatoes, sweet potato, and tiny pasta. Serves **6**. About 35 minutes of cooking.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 2 Tbsp | olive oil |
| 1 medium | yellow onion, diced |
| 3 stalks | celery, diced |
| 3 medium | carrots, diced |
| 3 cloves | garlic, minced |
| 1 Tbsp | tomato paste |
| 1 tsp | dried oregano |
| ½ tsp | dried thyme |
| 1 large | sweet potato, peeled and cut into ½-inch chunks |
| 1 can (14 oz) | diced tomatoes, with juices |
| 6 cups | vegetable broth |
| 1 | bay leaf |
| 1 cup | ditalini or other tiny pasta |
| 1 tsp | salt (a pinch goes in early, the rest at the end) |
| ½ tsp | black pepper |
| 2 Tbsp | fresh parsley, chopped |
| ¼ cup | grated Parmesan or Pecorino, for serving (optional) |

## Method

1. **Build the base (8 min).** Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, celery, and carrots with a pinch of the salt. Cook, stirring often, until softened.
2. **Add aromatics (2 min).** Stir in the garlic, tomato paste, oregano, and thyme. Cook until fragrant and the tomato paste darkens slightly.
3. **Simmer with tomatoes and sweet potato (15 min).** Add the sweet potato, tomatoes with their juices, broth, and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer, partially covered, until the sweet potato is just tender.
4. **Cook the pasta (9 min).** Stir in the pasta and cook until al dente, stirring occasionally so it doesn't stick. Remove the bay leaf.
5. **Season and serve.** Season with the remaining salt and the pepper, adjusting to taste. Ladle into bowls and top with parsley and cheese.

## Notes

- The pasta keeps absorbing broth as it sits. For leftovers, cook the pasta separately and add it to each bowl, or thin the soup with extra broth when reheating.
- Swap the sweet potato for cubed pumpkin or butternut squash.
- Stir in a handful of chopped kale or spinach in the last few minutes for extra greens.
- Skip the cheese (or use a vegetarian-friendly one) to keep it fully vegan.
